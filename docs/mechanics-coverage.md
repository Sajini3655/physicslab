## 2. NIE Grade 12 Canonical Practicals Specification

### Practical 2.1: Verification of the Parallelogram Law and Triangle of Forces
- **Source**: NIE Practical Handbook, Experiment 6 (Mechanics)
- **Apparatus**: Gravesande's force board with two low-friction pulleys, three slotted weight hangers ($P, Q, R$), string, drawing paper, mirror strip, protractor.
- **Physical Principle**:
  - Three coplanar concurrent forces in equilibrium: $\vec{P} + \vec{Q} + \vec{R} = 0$.
  - Resultant of $P$ and $Q$ must equal $R$ in magnitude and act directly opposite in direction.
- **Calculations & Graphing**:
  - Parallelogram diagonal force $F_{\text{diag}} = \sqrt{P^2 + Q^2 + 2PQ\cos\theta}$.
  - Percentage discrepancy: $|F_{\text{diag}} - R| / R \times 100\%$.
  - Triangle of forces vector loop closure.

### Practical 2.2: Verification of the Principle of Moments
- **Source**: NIE Practical Handbook, Experiment 7 (Mechanics)
- **Apparatus**: Uniform metre rule, knife-edge fulcrum, standard masses with loop hangers, spring balance.
- **Physical Principle**:
  - For a rigid body in rotational equilibrium: $\sum \tau_{\text{clockwise}} = \sum \tau_{\text{counter-clockwise}}$.
- **Calculations & Graphing**:
  - Balance condition: $m_1 g d_1 = m_2 g d_2 \implies m_1 d_1 = m_2 d_2$.
  - Determination of metre rule mass $M$: Knife edge at off-center position $x_0$, balancing known mass $m$ at distance $d_m \implies M \times |50.0 - x_0| = m \times d_m$.

### Practical 2.3: Determination of Relative Density of Immiscible Liquids using a U-Tube
- **Source**: NIE Practical Handbook, Experiment 8 (Mechanics)
- **Apparatus**: Glass U-tube clamped vertically, water, immiscible liquid (e.g., paraffin / coconut oil / mercury), metre rule, spirit level.
- **Physical Principle**:
  - At the common interface level (datum line), hydrostatic pressures in both limbs are equal:
    $$P_0 + h_1 \rho_1 g = P_0 + h_2 \rho_2 g \implies h_1 \rho_1 = h_2 \rho_2$$
  - Relative density: $\rho_{\text{rel}} = \frac{\rho_2}{\rho_1} = \frac{h_1}{h_2}$.

### Practical 2.4: Determination of Relative Density of Liquids using Hare’s Apparatus
- **Source**: NIE Practical Handbook, Experiment 9 (Mechanics)
- **Apparatus**: Hare's apparatus (two vertical tubes connected to a central T-piece with pinch cock and aspirator tube), two beakers, water, test liquid (brine / spirit), vertical millimeter scales.
- **Physical Principle**:
  - When air is partially evacuated via the aspirator, atmospheric pressure pushes liquids up both tubes:
    $$P_{\text{atm}} - P_{\text{inside}} = h_w \rho_w g = h_L \rho_L g \implies h_w \rho_w = h_L \rho_L$$
  - Linear relation: $h_w = \left(\frac{\rho_L}{\rho_w}\right) h_L$.
  - Graphing: Plotting $h_w$ against $h_L$ yields a straight line through the origin with gradient $m = \frac{\rho_L}{\rho_w} = \text{Relative Density}$.
  - Eliminates zero-level reading errors at liquid surfaces.

### Practical 2.5: Determination of Relative Density of Liquids using a Loaded Test Tube (Hydrometer)
- **Source**: NIE Practical Handbook, Experiment 10 (Mechanics)
- **Apparatus**: Uniform glass test tube loaded with lead shots / sand to float upright, millimeter scale inside tube, paraffin wax seal, tall jar of water, tall jar of test liquid, electronic balance.
- **Physical Principle**:
  - Law of Flotation: Weight of floating body = Weight of displaced liquid:
    $$M g = V_{\text{sub}} \rho g = A h \rho g \implies M = A h \rho$$
  - Constant mass method: Same loaded test tube floating in water to depth $h_w$ and liquid to depth $h_L$:
    $$A h_w \rho_w = A h_L \rho_L \implies \rho_{\text{rel}} = \frac{\rho_L}{\rho_w} = \frac{h_w}{h_L}$$
  - Graphing: Plotting immersion depth $h$ against mass $M$ in a given liquid: gradient $= \frac{1}{A\rho}$.
