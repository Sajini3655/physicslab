## 2. NIE Grade 12 Practicals Specification

### Practical 1: Determination of dimensions and volume of a hollow cylinder using Vernier Calipers
- **Apparatus**: Vernier calipers (least count 0.1 mm or 0.02 mm), hollow cylinder.
- **Physical Quantities**: External diameter ($D$), internal diameter ($d$), depth ($h$), external radius ($R = D/2$), internal radius ($r = d/2$).
- **Calculations**:
  - Zero error correction: $\text{Reading} = \text{Observed} - (\text{Zero Error})$.
  - Material volume: $V = \pi(R^2 - r^2)h$.
  - Internal cavity volume: $V_{\text{cavity}} = \pi r^2 h$.

### Practical 2: Determination of diameter and thickness of a coin using a Micrometer Screw Gauge
- **Apparatus**: Micrometer screw gauge (pitch 0.5 mm, 50 divisions, least count 0.01 mm), coin.
- **Physical Quantities**: Diameter ($D$), thickness ($t$).
- **Procedure & Error Avoidance**:
  - Zero error check (positive if zero is below reference line; negative if zero is above reference line).
  - Ratchet stop use to avoid deformation or pitch error.
  - Repeated measurements along multiple diameters to eliminate non-circularity error.
- **Calculations**:
  - $V = \pi (D/2)^2 t$.

### Practical 3: Determination of the radius of curvature of a spherical surface using a Spherometer
- **Apparatus**: Spherometer (pitch 1 mm, 100 circular divisions, least count 0.01 mm), plane glass plate, convex spherical surface / concave mirror, millimeter scale.
- **Physical Quantities**: Distance between outer legs ($a$), height/sagitta ($h$).
- **Mathematical Formula**:
  $$R = \frac{a^2}{6h} + \frac{h}{2}$$
- **Safeguard**: When $h \to 0$ (plane surface), $R \to \infty$. Prevent division by zero.

### Practical 4: Determination of internal and external radii of a rubber tube using a Travelling Microscope
- **Apparatus**: Travelling microscope (horizontal and vertical vernier scales, least count 0.01 mm), rubber tube section, illumination source.
- **Physical Quantities**: Left outer edge ($x_1$), left inner edge ($x_2$), right inner edge ($x_3$), right outer edge ($x_4$).
- **Calculations**:
  - Internal diameter: $d_{\text{int}} = x_3 - x_2 \implies r_{\text{int}} = d_{\text{int}}/2$.
  - External diameter: $d_{\text{ext}} = x_4 - x_1 \implies r_{\text{ext}} = d_{\text{ext}}/2$.
  - Wall thickness: $t = r_{\text{ext}} - r_{\text{int}}$.

### Practical 5: Determination of the density of a regular solid
- **Apparatus**: Beam/electronic balance, vernier calipers or micrometer screw gauge (depending on specimen size), regular solid (rectangular block / cylinder / sphere).
- **Physical Quantities**: Mass ($m$), length ($l$), width ($w$), thickness ($t$) or diameter ($d$).
- **Calculations & Error Propagation**:
  - Density: $\rho = \frac{m}{V}$.
  - Unit conversion: $\text{g cm}^{-3} \times 1000 \implies \text{kg m}^{-3}$.
  - Fractional error: $\frac{\Delta \rho}{\rho} = \frac{\Delta m}{m} + \frac{\Delta l}{l} + \frac{\Delta w}{w} + \frac{\Delta t}{t}$.
