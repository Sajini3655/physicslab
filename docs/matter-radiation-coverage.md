# Unit 11: Matter and Radiation (පදාර්ථය සහ විකිරණ) - Syllabus & Curriculum Coverage

## Sri Lankan G.C.E. (Advanced Level) Physics Specification

### 1. Competency Breakdown

#### Competency 11.1: Photoelectric Effect & Photon Theory of Radiation (ප්‍රකාශ විද්‍යුත් ආචරණය සහ ෆෝටෝන වාදය)
- **Failure of Classical Wave Theory:**
  - Inability to explain threshold frequency ($f_0$): Classical theory predicted emission at any frequency given sufficient light intensity.
  - Inability to explain instantaneous emission ($< 10^{-9}\text{ s}$): Classical wave energy is distributed over wavefront; accumulation time would be hours.
  - Inability to explain maximum kinetic energy independence of intensity: Maximum kinetic energy depends solely on photon frequency, not wave amplitude/intensity.
- **Planck's Quantum Hypothesis:**
  - Radiant energy is emitted and absorbed in discrete quanta called photons:
    $$E = hf = \frac{hc}{\lambda}$$
    where $h = 6.626 \times 10^{-34}\text{ J s}$, $c = 3.0 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$.
  - Photon momentum:
    $$p = \frac{E}{c} = \frac{h}{\lambda}$$
- **Einstein's Photoelectric Equation:**
  $$hf = \Phi + K_{\max} = hf_0 + \frac{1}{2}m_e v_{\max}^2$$
  - Work Function ($\Phi$): Minimum energy required to liberate an electron from metal surface:
    $$\Phi = hf_0 = \frac{hc}{\lambda_0}$$
- **Stopping Potential ($V_s$):**
  $$e V_s = K_{\max} = hf - \Phi \implies V_s = \left(\frac{h}{e}\right)f - \frac{\Phi}{e}$$
  - Plot of $V_s$ vs $f$: Straight line with slope $h/e$ (universal constant for all metals) and x-intercept $f_0$.
- **Photocurrent Characteristics:**
  - Saturation current is directly proportional to incident light intensity.
  - Stopping potential is independent of intensity but increases linearly with frequency.

#### Competency 11.2: Wave-Particle Duality, de Broglie Waves & X-Rays (තරංග-අංශු ද්වෛතය, ඩි බ්‍රොග්ලි තරංග සහ එක්ස්-කිරණ)
- **De Broglie Hypothesis:**
  - Matter exhibits dual particle and wave properties:
    $$\lambda = \frac{h}{p} = \frac{h}{mv}$$
  - For a particle of mass $m$ and charge $q$ accelerated through potential difference $V$:
    $$E_k = qV \implies p = \sqrt{2m q V} \implies \lambda = \frac{h}{\sqrt{2m q V}}$$
  - For an electron ($m_e = 9.11 \times 10^{-31}\text{ kg}, e = 1.6 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C}$):
    $$\lambda \approx \frac{1.227}{\sqrt{V}}\text{ nm}$$
- **Experimental Verification:**
  - Davisson-Germer electron diffraction from nickel crystal lattices.
  - Transmission electron diffraction through thin polycrystalline graphite foils demonstrating circular diffraction rings.
- **X-Rays:**
  - Production in Coolidge tube via high-energy electron bombardment of heavy metal target (tungsten/molybdenum).
  - Continuous spectrum (Bremsstrahlung / braking radiation):
    - Duane-Hunt limit: Maximum photon energy equals full kinetic energy of accelerating electrons ($eV_a$):
      $$E_{\max} = hf_{\max} = \frac{hc}{\lambda_{\min}} = e V_a \implies \lambda_{\min} = \frac{hc}{e V_a}$$
  - Characteristic X-ray spectrum:
    - Discrete sharp emission lines ($K_\alpha, K_\beta, L_\alpha$) arising from inner-shell electron transitions following core ionization.

#### Competency 11.3: Atomic Models & Line Emission Spectra (පරමාණුක ආකෘති සහ රේඛීය විමෝචන වර්ණාවලි)
- **Rutherford Nuclear Model:**
  - Alpha particle scattering through thin gold foil. Most pass undeviated; a small fraction undergo large-angle back-scattering.
  - Nucleus is positively charged, extremely dense ($r \approx 10^{-14}\text{ m}$), containing nearly all atomic mass.
  - Limitation: Classical electrodynamics predicts accelerating orbiting electrons emit radiation continuously and spiral into nucleus in $\approx 10^{-11}\text{ s}$.
- **Bohr's Postulates for Hydrogen Atom:**
  1. Electrons orbit the nucleus in non-radiating stable stationary orbits.
  2. Quantization of orbital angular momentum:
     $$L = m_e v r = \frac{n h}{2\pi}, \quad n = 1, 2, 3, \dots$$
  3. Photon emission/absorption occurs only during quantum transitions between stationary states:
     $$\Delta E = E_2 - E_1 = hf = \frac{hc}{\lambda}$$
- **Energy Levels of Hydrogen:**
  $$E_n = -\frac{13.6}{n^2}\text{ eV} = -\frac{2.18 \times 10^{-18}}{n^2}\text{ J}$$
  - Ground state ($n=1$): $E_1 = -13.6\text{ eV}$. Ionization energy of hydrogen is $13.6\text{ eV}$.
- **Hydrogen Spectral Series & Rydberg Formula:**
  $$\frac{1}{\lambda} = R_H \left(\frac{1}{n_1^2} - \frac{1}{n_2^2}\right)$$
  where $R_H = 1.097 \times 10^7\text{ m}^{-1}$.
  - **Lyman Series:** $n_1 = 1, n_2 = 2, 3, 4, \dots$ (Ultraviolet)
  - **Balmer Series:** $n_1 = 2, n_2 = 3, 4, 5, \dots$ (Visible spectrum: $H_\alpha = 656.3\text{ nm}$ red, $H_\beta = 486.1\text{ nm}$ blue-green, $H_\gamma = 434.0\text{ nm}$ violet)
  - **Paschen Series:** $n_1 = 3, n_2 = 4, 5, 6, \dots$ (Infrared)
  - **Brackett Series:** $n_1 = 4, n_2 = 5, 6, \dots$ (Far Infrared)
  - **Pfund Series:** $n_1 = 5, n_2 = 6, 7, \dots$ (Far Infrared)

#### Competency 11.4: Nuclear Physics & Radioactivity (න්‍යෂ්ටික භෞතික විද්‍යාව සහ විකිරණශීලීතාව)
- **Nuclear Structure:**
  - Atomic number $Z$ (protons), Mass number $A$ (nucleons), Neutron number $N = A - Z$.
  - Nuclear radius empirical relationship:
    $$R = R_0 A^{1/3}, \quad R_0 \approx 1.2 \times 10^{-15}\text{ m} = 1.2\text{ fm}$$
  - Nuclear density is constant across all nuclei:
    $$\rho = \frac{M}{V} = \frac{A \cdot u}{\frac{4}{3}\pi (R_0 A^{1/3})^3} \approx 2.3 \times 10^{17}\text{ kg/m}^3$$
- **Mass Defect & Binding Energy:**
  - Mass defect $\Delta m$:
    $$\Delta m = [Z m_p + (A - Z) m_n] - M_{\text{nucleus}}$$
  - Binding energy:
    $$BE = \Delta m \cdot c^2 = \Delta m (\text{in u}) \times 931.5\text{ MeV}$$
  - Binding Energy per Nucleon ($BE/A$) Curve:
    - Rises sharply from light nuclei, reaches maximum peak at $^{56}\text{Fe}$ ($\approx 8.8\text{ MeV/nucleon}$), then gently declines for heavy nuclei ($\approx 7.6\text{ MeV/nucleon}$ at $^{238}\text{U}$).
    - Fission: Heavy nuclei splitting yields fragments closer to peak, releasing energy.
    - Fusion: Light nuclei combining yields products closer to peak, releasing even higher energy per unit mass.
- **Law of Radioactive Decay:**
  $$N(t) = N_0 e^{-\lambda t}$$
  - Activity $A(t) = -\frac{dN}{dt} = \lambda N(t) = A_0 e^{-\lambda t}$ (SI unit: Becquerel, $\text{Bq} = 1\text{ decay/s}$).
  - Half-life ($T_{1/2}$):
    $$T_{1/2} = \frac{\ln 2}{\lambda} \approx \frac{0.693}{\lambda}$$
- **Emissions:**
  - $\alpha$ decay ($^4_2\text{He}$ nucleus, high ionization, low penetration).
  - $\beta^-$ decay ($e^- + \bar{\nu}_e$, moderate ionization and penetration).
  - $\beta^+$ decay ($e^+ + \nu_e$).
  - $\gamma$ decay (high-energy photon, low ionization, high penetration).
