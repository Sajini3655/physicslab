# Unit 4: Thermal Physics (තාප භෞතික විද්‍යාව) - Syllabus & Practical Coverage Map

## 1. Syllabus Competencies (NIE A/L Physics)

- **Competency 4.1: Temperature and Thermometry (උෂ්ණත්වය සහ උෂ්ණත්වමිතිය)**
  - Concept of temperature, thermal equilibrium, and Zeroth Law of Thermodynamics.
  - Thermometric properties and linear calibration ($t = \frac{X_t - X_0}{X_{100} - X_0} \times 100^\circ\text{C}$).
  - Types of thermometers: Liquid-in-glass (mercury/alcohol), constant-volume gas thermometer ($T = \frac{P}{P_{tr}} \times 273.16\text{ K}$), platinum resistance thermometer ($R_t = R_0(1 + \alpha t)$), thermoelectric pyrometer (Seebeck effect).
  - Absolute thermodynamic temperature scale (Kelvin).

- **Competency 4.2: Thermal Expansion (තාප ප්‍රසාරණය)**
  - Linear expansivity $\alpha$ ($L_t = L_0(1 + \alpha \Delta t)$), superficial expansivity $\beta \approx 2\alpha$, cubical expansivity $\gamma \approx 3\alpha$.
  - Expansion of liquids: Real cubical expansivity $\gamma_r$ vs apparent expansivity $\gamma_a$ ($\gamma_r = \gamma_a + \gamma_g$).
  - Anomalous expansion of water and ecological significance (maximum density at $4^\circ\text{C}$).

- **Competency 4.3: Heat Capacity and Latent Heat (තාප ධාරිතාව සහ ගුප්ත තාපය)**
  - Specific heat capacity $c$ ($Q = mc\Delta \theta$), heat capacity $C = mc$, water equivalent.
  - Latent heat of fusion $L_f$ and vaporization $L_v$ ($Q = mL$).
  - Method of mixtures with radiation/cooling corrections.
  - Newton's law of cooling: $\frac{d\theta}{dt} = -k(\theta - \theta_0)$.

- **Competency 4.4: Gas Laws and Kinetic Theory (වායු නියම සහ චාලක වාදය)**
  - Boyle's law ($PV = \text{const}$ at constant $T$), Charles's law ($V/T = \text{const}$ at constant $P$), Pressure law ($P/T = \text{const}$).
  - Ideal gas equation of state: $PV = nRT = NkT$.
  - Kinetic theory derivation: $P = \frac{1}{3} \rho \overline{c^2} = \frac{1}{3} \frac{Nm}{V} \overline{c^2}$.
  - Translational kinetic energy per molecule: $\frac{1}{2}m\overline{c^2} = \frac{3}{2}kT$.
  - Root-mean-square speed: $c_{rms} = \sqrt{\frac{3RT}{M}}$.

- **Competency 4.5: Heat Transfer and Thermodynamics (තාප සම්ප්‍රේෂණය සහ තාපගති විද්‍යාව)**
  - Conduction: Rate of heat flow $\frac{dQ}{dt} = -kA \frac{d\theta}{dx}$.
  - Searle's bar method for thermal conductivity $k$.
  - Convection currents and atmospheric circulation.
  - Radiation: Prevost's theory of heat exchanges, blackbody radiation, Stefan-Boltzmann law ($E = \sigma T^4$), Wien's displacement law ($\lambda_{max} T = b$).
  - First Law of Thermodynamics: $\Delta Q = \Delta U + \Delta W$ (with sign conventions).
  - Thermodynamic processes: Isothermal ($\Delta U = 0, W = nRT \ln(V_2/V_1)$), Adiabatic ($PV^\gamma = \text{const}, \Delta Q = 0$), Isobaric ($W = P\Delta V$), Isochoric ($W = 0$).

## 2. Canonical Laboratory Practicals (NIE Practical Handbook)

1. **Practical 4.1: Verification of Boyle’s Law for a Gas**
   - Apparatus: Boyle's law apparatus or Quill tube with mercury index pellet.
   - Equation: $P \cdot V = \text{constant}$ or $P$ vs $1/L$ linear plot through origin.
   - Total pressure: $P = H \pm h$ where $H$ is atmospheric pressure and $h$ is mercury column height difference.

2. **Practical 4.2: Specific Heat Capacity of a Metal by Method of Mixtures**
   - Apparatus: Copper calorimeter with stirrer, lagging, thermometer, boiler with steam jacket.
   - Equation: $m_s c_s (\theta_2 - \theta_3) = (m_c c_c + m_w c_w)(\theta_3 - \theta_1) + \text{radiation loss}$.

3. **Practical 4.3: Specific Latent Heat of Fusion of Ice**
   - Apparatus: Calorimeter, water, dry crushed ice, sensitive balance, thermometer.
   - Equation: $m_{ice} L_f + m_{ice} c_w (\theta_f - 0) = (m_c c_c + m_w c_w)(\theta_i - \theta_f)$.

4. **Practical 4.4: Specific Latent Heat of Vaporization of Water**
   - Apparatus: Boiler with water trap, steam delivery tube, calorimeter with cold water.
   - Equation: $m_{steam} L_v + m_{steam} c_w (100 - \theta_f) = (m_c c_c + m_w c_w)(\theta_f - \theta_i)$.

5. **Practical 4.5: Verification of Newton’s Law of Cooling**
   - Apparatus: Copper calorimeter with hot water, stirrer, thermometer, stop watch, constant temperature enclosure.
   - Equation: Plot of cooling curve $\theta$ vs $t$, tangents $\frac{d\theta}{dt}$, verify $\frac{d\theta}{dt} \propto (\theta - \theta_0)$.

6. **Practical 4.6: Thermal Conductivity of a Good Conductor (Searle’s Bar)**
   - Apparatus: Searle's insulated copper bar, steam chamber, cold water circulating coil, 4 thermometers.
   - Equation: Heat flow rate $k A \frac{\theta_1 - \theta_2}{d} = \frac{m_w c_w (\theta_4 - \theta_3)}{t}$.
