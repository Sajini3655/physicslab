## Canonical Physical Constants & Standard Formulas

- Acceleration due to gravity: $g = 9.8\text{ m s}^{-2}$ (or $9.81\text{ m s}^{-2}$)
- Density of water: $\rho_w = 1000\text{ kg m}^{-3}$
- Surface tension of pure water at $20^\circ\text{C}$: $\gamma_w \approx 0.0728\text{ N m}^{-1}$
- Hooke's Law: $F = k \Delta L$, with stiffness $k = \frac{YA}{L}$
- Young's modulus: $Y = \frac{FL}{A \Delta L} = \frac{4FL}{\pi d^2 \Delta L}$
- Strain energy: $U = \frac{1}{2} F \Delta L = \frac{1}{2} \frac{YA}{L} (\Delta L)^2$
- Poiseuille flow: $Q = \frac{\pi P r^4}{8 \eta L}$
- Stokes' Law: $F_d = 6\pi \eta r v$
- Terminal velocity: $v_t = \frac{2 r^2 (\rho - \sigma) g}{9\eta}$
- Excess drop pressure: $\Delta P = \frac{2\gamma}{R}$
- Excess bubble pressure: $\Delta P = \frac{4\gamma}{R}$
- Capillary rise: $h = \frac{2\gamma \cos\theta}{\rho g r}$
