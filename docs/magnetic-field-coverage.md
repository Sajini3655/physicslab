## Canonical Physical Constants & Standard SI Values

- Permeability of free space: $\mu_0 = 4\pi \times 10^{-7}\text{ T m A}^{-1}$ (or $\text{H m}^{-1} \approx 1.256637 \times 10^{-6}\text{ N A}^{-2}$)
- Speed of light in vacuum: $c = 1/\sqrt{\varepsilon_0 \mu_0} \approx 2.99792 \times 10^8\text{ m s}^{-1}$
- Electron charge magnitude: $e = 1.602176634 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C}$
- Electron mass: $m_e = 9.10938 \times 10^{-31}\text{ kg}$
- Proton mass: $m_p = 1.67262 \times 10^{-27}\text{ kg}$
