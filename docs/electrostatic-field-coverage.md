## Canonical Physical Constants & Standard SI Values

- Vacuum permittivity: $\varepsilon_0 = 8.8541878128 \times 10^{-12}\text{ F m}^{-1}$ (or $\text{C}^2 \text{N}^{-1}\text{ m}^{-2}$)
- Coulomb constant: $k_e = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \approx 8.98755 \times 10^9\text{ N m}^2 \text{C}^{-2}$
- Elementary charge: $e = 1.602176634 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C}$
- Electron mass: $m_e = 9.10938 \times 10^{-31}\text{ kg}$
- Proton mass: $m_p = 1.67262 \times 10^{-27}\text{ kg}$
