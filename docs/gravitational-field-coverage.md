## 1. Syllabus Competencies & Learning Outcomes

| Competency | Official Syllabus Topic | Key Theoretical Principles | Practical / Computational Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **5.1** | **Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation** | • $F = G \frac{M m}{r^2}$<br>• Universal gravitational constant $G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11}\text{ N m}^2\text{ kg}^{-2}$<br>• Point masses and spherically symmetric mass distributions<br>• Superposition of gravitational forces in multi-body configurations | Two-body force calculations, Cavendish torsion balance principle, vector resultant of gravitational attractions |
| **5.2** | **Gravitational Field Intensity ($g$)** | • Definition: $g = \frac{F}{m} = \frac{GM}{r^2}$<br>• Variation with altitude $h$: $g_h = \frac{GM}{(R+h)^2} = g_0 \left(1 + \frac{h}{R}\right)^{-2} \approx g_0 \left(1 - \frac{2h}{R}\right)$ for $h \ll R$<br>• Variation with depth $d$: $g_d = g_0 \left(1 - \frac{d}{R}\right)$ for uniform density<br>• Field intensity at center of Earth is zero ($g = 0$)<br>• Gravitational field lines and equipotential surfaces | Altitude and depth variation curves, neutral points between Earth and Moon ($g_{net} = 0$) |
| **5.3** | **Gravitational Potential ($V$) & Potential Energy ($U$)** | • Potential defined as work done per unit mass from infinity: $V = -\frac{GM}{r}$ ($V_\infty = 0$)<br>• Potential energy: $U = -\frac{GMm}{r}$<br>• Work done moving mass between two points: $W = m(V_B - V_A)$<br>• Escape velocity from planetary surface: $v_e = \sqrt{\frac{2GM}{R}} = \sqrt{2gR}$ ($v_e \approx 11.2\text{ km/s}$ for Earth) | Work required to launch projectiles to infinity, escape velocity dependencies on planetary radius and mass |
| **5.4** | **Satellite Motion & Orbital Dynamics** | • Circular orbital speed: $v_o = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{r}} = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{R+h}}$<br>• Orbital period: $T = \frac{2\pi r}{v_o} = \frac{2\pi r^{3/2}}{\sqrt{GM}}$<br>• Kepler's Third Law: $T^2 = \left(\frac{4\pi^2}{GM}\right) r^3$<br>• Geostationary satellite conditions: circular, equatorial plane, west-to-east orbit, period $T = 24\text{ h}$, altitude $h \approx 3.59 \times 10^4\text{ km}$ ($r \approx 4.22 \times 10^4\text{ km}$)<br>• Total mechanical energy: $E = KE + PE = \frac{GMm}{2r} - \frac{GMm}{r} = -\frac{GMm}{2r}$<br>• Apparent weightlessness in orbit ($R_N = 0$) | Interactive satellite orbital mechanics simulation, geostationary synchronization, energy changes during orbital transfer |

## 2. Standard Physical Constants

- **Universal Gravitational Constant ($G$):** $6.674 \times 10^{-11}\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$
- **Mass of Earth ($M_E$):** $5.972 \times 10^{24}\text{ kg}$
- **Radius of Earth ($R_E$):** $6.371 \times 10^6\text{ m} \approx 6400\text{ km}$
- **Standard Surface Gravity ($g_0$):** $9.807\text{ m/s}^2$
- **Earth Escape Velocity ($v_e$):** $1.119 \times 10^4\text{ m/s} \approx 11.2\text{ km/s}$
- **Mass of Moon ($M_M$):** $7.348 \times 10^{22}\text{ kg}$
- **Earth-Moon Center Distance ($d_{EM}$):** $3.844 \times 10^8\text{ m}$
